## Description
Right after a game, you want to know one thing: what could have gone better?
Rookie Lab turns every game into a review you can follow, so you understand each one a little more.

[Enter a username, start reviewing]
Type your Chess.com or Lichess username and your recent games show up automatically. You can also tap Share in the Chess.com or Lichess app and send a game straight to Rookie Lab. No sign-up, no login.

[Quick review: pauses at the key moments]
After analysis, the game plays through quickly with a rating on every move. On a great move it pauses and tells you why it works. On a missed chance it pauses and lets you look for something better. If a move didn't go as planned, you see how your opponent would respond first, then try again.

[Every move explained]
Beyond "best move", you get plain wording: fork, material gain, defense, escaping an attack. You see what the move did and how it compares. Want more depth? Compare two or three candidate moves two moves ahead, or play moves out on the board yourself.

[5 daily puzzles, built from your own games]
Missed chances in your reviews turn into practice puzzles, with the game they came from and what your opponent just played. Spaced repetition does the scheduling: puzzles you're still learning return after a few questions, and ones you know come back after a few days. A puzzle graduates once it truly sticks.

[Multi-game report]
See your last 10 games at once: accuracy, performance by phase, recurring patterns, time usage, and what to practice first.

[Endgame lessons]
Learn the key idea of a common endgame, follow a step-by-step demo, then practice against the engine until it clicks.

[Analysis on your phone]
The analysis engine runs right on your phone. Your games and practice history are stored on the device.

Rookie Lab is for anyone getting serious about chess and wondering how to improve. Finish a game, then come take a look!
